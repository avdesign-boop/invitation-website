# Marketing, Sign-up, Login, Plan Selection

## Screens
Landing page, pricing, sign-up, login, device verification (enter the emailed code), forgot password, plan picker.

## Login page settings
The login page hides the sign-up link when sign-up is off. It shows a login hint, the account with a button that fills it in, when `LOGIN_HINT_EMAIL` and `LOGIN_HINT_PASSWORD` are set.
